Installation

Set up your Mac build

  1. Open the GitHub Releases page and choose the macOS package that matches your Mac: Apple Silicon or Intel.
  2. Download the available DMG or ZIP. Open a DMG and follow its included layout, or extract the ZIP archive.
  3. Open the Ape Escape Recompiled app from the extracted or mounted package. Keep any accompanying application files in place.
  4. Choose your own Ape Escape disc image in the launcher. A CUE file with its matching BIN track is recommended; CHD is also supported.
  5. Configure your controller and display settings, then start the game.
macOS security notice: The project's Mac app is ad-hoc signed rather than notarized. macOS may therefore ask you to approve opening it. Only open a build you downloaded from the project's official releases.

Apple Silicon and Intel

Choose the package labelled for your Mac's processor architecture. Apple Silicon builds are intended for M-series Macs; Intel builds are for Intel-based Macs. Do not assume that a package for one architecture is interchangeable with the other.

Your game data and saves

The original game is not included. Supply your own legally obtained disc image. OpenBIOS is the default BIOS alternative; a retail SCPH-1001 BIOS is optional if you have one and choose to use it.

Settings and saves are kept outside the macOS app bundle. Replacing the app should not require deleting your per-user data folder; avoid removing it when updating.
