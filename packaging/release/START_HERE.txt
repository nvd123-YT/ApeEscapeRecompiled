ApeEscapeRecomp - Ape Escape, recompiled
========================================

WHAT YOU NEED
  Your own Ape Escape (USA, SCUS-94423) disc image. A .cue + .bin pair is
  preferred; pick the .cue when both are present. The disc is NOT included.

  A PlayStation BIOS is NOT required. This package bundles the MIT-licensed
  OpenBIOS from PCSX-Redux (bios/openbios.bin, notice in bios/OpenBIOS.LICENSE).
  You may optionally select your own legally obtained SCPH1001.BIN instead.

FIRST RUN
  1. Launch ApeEscapeRecomp.exe. The launcher window opens.
  2. Set the Disc row to your Ape Escape image.
  3. Visit Settings for Renderer (Software/OpenGL/Vulkan when available),
     render/present threading, and VSync options including Adaptive/VRR when
     supported by your display and backend.
  4. Visit Mods for image filtering, Internal Resolution (4x fresh default;
     Game default follows this title's 4K setting), and optional Ape-specific
     enhancements. Widescreen remains off by default; when enabled, Fit to
     screen follows the window aspect.
     For PS1 dithering, enable Mods > Image Filtering, then choose Dithering:
     Game default, Off, Native (PS1), or Fine. Dithering is OpenGL-only; the
     choice is retained for the next OpenGL session on other renderers.
  5. In Controls, Quick Save and Quick Load can be assigned to your preferred
     keys or controller buttons. Press Launch when ready. Your choices persist.

SAVE STATES AND REWIND
  Save states and rewind are available through the launcher hotkeys. Defaults:
  F7 opens the save-state menu and F8 rewinds. Memory cards, save states, and
  rewind data are stored under saves/.

WHERE THINGS LIVE
  saves/     memory cards, save states, and rewind metadata
  cache/     converted native code, grows as you play
  mods/      bundled and user-installed mod packages
  input.ini  controller mapping (yours after first launch)

PRIVACY NOTE
  overlay_captures.json and the cache folder contain code converted from
  YOUR disc. Do not redistribute them.

TROUBLESHOOTING
  Slow performance: lower Supersampling to 1 in the launcher.
  Controller not detected: check input.ini, or use the launcher's controller
  row to pick the device.
